Permission-Slip & Field-Trip Log
One line per trip: signed, paid, chaperoning, and what to pack.
How to use this sheet

Every time a permission slip comes home, write one line here before you sign it — the Cost and Paid columns are where the real surprises happen. When the trip is over, mark the last box and stop thinking about it. This log doubles as a memory keeper: years later, these lines read like a little scrapbook of zoo trips and pumpkin patches.
