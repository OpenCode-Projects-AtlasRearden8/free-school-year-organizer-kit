Backpack Folder Map
A simple color-code system, so paperwork stops living in a black hole.
How to use this sheet

Buy four cheap two-pocket folders (or reuse last year's) and a bunch of sticky labels. Label and color-code them like this sheet, then fill in the blanks with the real names from your child's school. The rule that makes it work: papers only move right to left. They arrive in TO SCHOOL, get handled, and end up in HOME when they have nowhere to go. Empty the folders every school night on the checklist spot (sheet 1 covers weeks one to two; this map covers the whole year).

Folder Color / label What goes in it
TO SCHOOL (left pocket) _______________ Papers headed back: forms, signed notes, teacher replies — the “outgoing mail” of the backpack.
HOME (right pocket) _______________ Anything that stays home after the nightly empty-out.
MONEY & FORMS _______________ Lunch money (see the lunch card, sheet 6), book-order cash, anything with a due date. Check it every night.
KEEP ALL YEAR _______________ Schedules, login cards, class list, report cards — cleared into a home binder at each break (sheet 8 finishes the year).
Nightly backpack empty-out — read it in order, it takes two minutes:

1. Lunchbox out, wipe it    2. WATER BOTTLE emptied (tomorrow's you says thanks)    3. Paper from HOME pocket, glanced and sorted

4. Sign / fill anything in MONEY & FORMS    5. Papers back into TO SCHOOL    6. Homework into HOME, then into kid's homework spot
