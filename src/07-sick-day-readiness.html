How to use this sheet

This is a “do it once, use it all year” sheet. On a calm Sunday, look up the numbers and fill in the top half — it takes ten minutes and saves you from a 6:15 a.m. scramble. The bottom half is a log: one line per sick day, so at year's end you know exactly how many days were used vs. allowed. This is a household organizer, not medical advice — your school nurse and doctor are the real experts; this just keeps the boring logistics on rails.
