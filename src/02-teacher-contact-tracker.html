Who to reach, how to reach them, and what was said — no more digging through email.
How to use this sheet

Fill one row per adult worth knowing: homeroom teacher, specials teachers, bus driver, school nurse, counselor, front office. Keep this sheet in the front of your home-school binder (see the backpack map, sheet 3). When you call or email, note the date and a one-line summary in “Last contact” — two words is enough. If a conversation ever needs a follow-up, you will be glad you wrote it down.
