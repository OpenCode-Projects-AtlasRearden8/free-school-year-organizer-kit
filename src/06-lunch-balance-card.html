Lunch Balance Sweep Card
A tiny monthly habit that stops the “your child owes $23” email.
How to use this card

One school-night a month (payday? trash day? the first Monday?) spend five minutes on this card. Log in to the district lunch portal, peek at the balance for each kid, refill if it is low, and jot the date and amount on a line below. If you ever pack lunches instead, circle PB and reset next month. Pin this card inside a cabinet door by the snacks — the snackiest place in the house.
