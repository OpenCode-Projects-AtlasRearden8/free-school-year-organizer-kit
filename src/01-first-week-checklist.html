How to use this sheet

Put this on the fridge or next to your coffee maker during the first week of school. Every time a folder comes home, jot the due date in the blank for anything that applies, then check the box when it is handled. Kids bring paperwork in waves — when something new lands, add it to the blank lines at the bottom. Toss it in a drawer when it is done — the rest of the kit takes over from here.
