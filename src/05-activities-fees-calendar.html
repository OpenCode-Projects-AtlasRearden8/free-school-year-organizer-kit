How to use this sheet

Copy dates off flyers, the district website, and that one crumpled note from the folder when the season starts. Fall and winter versions are on the front; spring on the back. The point is not a perfect calendar — your phone already does that. The point is the cost column: seeing all the fees in one place means no surprise $200 month in March. Add a total at each season's end and you will budget next year in five minutes.
